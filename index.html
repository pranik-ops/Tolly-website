Frequently asked questions

What age is Tolly for?

Tolly is designed for children roughly 3 to 10, and adapts as your child grows.

Is it a screen?

No. Tolly is voice-only, on purpose — no screen, ever.

Will it replace me as a parent?

No. Tolly is built to extend your presence, not replace it. You always set the direction and deliver your guidance.

Is my child's data safe?

Safety and privacy are foundational to how Tolly is built. We'll share full details as we get closer to launch.

How much will it cost / when can I get it?

We're still in early development. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know as things take shape.

What can Tolly actually do?

Tolly listens, talks, and explores the world alongside your child — we're keeping the details under wraps while we refine the experience with early families. You'll see more as we get closer to launch.
